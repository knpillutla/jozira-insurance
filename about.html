About Jozira

Jozira was created to help travelers and visitors understand medical insurance options without confusion or pressure. We focus on clear information, transparency, and honest guidance so users can make informed decisions.

Our Mission

What We Do

Jozira helps you understand and compare USA visitor medical insurance plans. We:

  • Research Plans: We continuously update information about insurance plans suitable for international visitors
  • Simplify Information: We explain coverage options, benefits, and limitations in clear language
  • Provide Comparisons: We help you compare plans based on your specific travel needs, dates, and age
  • Direct Connection: We provide direct links to insurance providers so you can purchase with confidence

What We Don't Do

Jozira is not an insurance company or agent. We do not:

  • Sell insurance directly
  • Handle insurance claims
  • Provide professional insurance or legal advice
  • Collect payments or process transactions
  • Store sensitive personal information like policy numbers or payment details

Who We Serve

Jozira is designed for:

  • International Parents: Visiting family in the USA
  • Business Travelers: On B1/B2 visas
  • Tourists: Taking short vacations to the USA
  • Students: Traveling or studying abroad
  • Long-term Visitors: Needing coverage for extended stays

How We Make Money

Jozira may earn a commission through affiliate partnerships when users purchase insurance through our links.

This means:

  • You pay the same price whether you buy directly from the provider or through our link
  • The insurance company pays us a commission for referrals
  • This does not affect your insurance cost or coverage in any way
  • Our recommendation of insurance partners is independent and unbiased
