What to Look For in Insurance Plans

Coverage Duration

Choose a plan that covers your entire stay in the USA. Most plans offer coverage from 5 days to 364 days, depending on your needs.

Coverage Amounts

Look at the maximum coverage amount for hospital stays and emergency care. Higher coverage provides better protection against unexpected costs.

Deductible & Co-payments

Understand your deductible (the amount you pay before insurance kicks in) and co-payments (what you pay for each service). Lower deductibles often mean higher premiums.

Pre-existing Conditions

Check whether the plan covers pre-existing medical conditions. Some plans include them automatically, while others may exclude or require additional premium.

Network Providers

Verify that the plan covers hospitals and doctors in the areas you'll be visiting in the USA.
