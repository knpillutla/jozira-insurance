Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us.

We collect limited information to improve website functionality and user experience. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly

  • Contact Form: When you contact us using our contact form, we collect your name, email address, and message content
  • Comparison Form: When you use our insurance comparison tool, we collect your trip dates, age, and travel type to help compare plans
  • Email Communications: If you sign up for updates or newsletters, we collect your email address

Information Collected Automatically

  • Cookies: We use cookies and similar technologies to track affiliate referrals and understand how visitors use our website
  • Logs: Our servers automatically log information such as your IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time spent on our site
  • Analytics: We use analytics tools to track visitor behavior and improve our website's functionality

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Service Improvement: To understand how you use our website and improve functionality
  • Affiliate Tracking: To track affiliate referrals when you click insurance provider links and to ensure proper commission accounting
  • Communication: To respond to your inquiries and maintain contact with you
  • Analytics: To measure website performance and user behavior
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable laws and regulations

3. Third-Party Links and Privacy

Our website may contain third-party affiliate links. When you click these links, you are subject to the privacy policies of the respective third-party providers. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites. Please review their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

4. Information We Do NOT Store

Jozira does not collect or store sensitive personal information such as:

  • Payment details or credit card information
  • Insurance policy numbers
  • Government-issued identification numbers (SSN, passport, etc.)
  • Health or medical records
  • Bank account information

We do not process payments on our website. All insurance purchases are handled directly by the insurance providers.

5. Information Sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties except in the following cases:

  • Affiliate Tracking: Limited information (such as your trip dates and age) may be shared with insurance providers when you click their affiliate links for comparison and referral purposes only
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information when required by law, court order, or government request
  • Service Providers: We may share information with trusted service providers who help us operate our website (e.g., hosting providers, analytics companies), under strict confidentiality agreements

Personal information is not sold or shared for marketing purposes.

6. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Track affiliate referrals and ensure proper attribution
  • Remember your preferences and settings
  • Understand how you interact with our website
  • Improve our website's performance and user experience

You can control cookie settings in your browser. If you disable cookies, some features of our website may not function properly.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, and disclosure. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to provide our services and fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy. You can request deletion of your information at any time by contacting us.

9. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights:

  • Access: You can request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction: You can request corrections to inaccurate information
  • Deletion: You can request that we delete your personal information
  • Opt-Out: You can opt out of marketing communications at any time

To exercise these rights, please contact us using our contact form.

10. Age Requirement

This website is intended for users aged 18 years and above. We do not intentionally collect personal information from individuals under 18. If we become aware that we have collected information from someone under 18, we will delete such information immediately.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Your continued use of our website constitutes acceptance of our updated policy.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us. We're committed to protecting your privacy.

For EU/GDPR Users: If you are located in the European Union, your personal information is processed in accordance with GDPR requirements. You have additional rights under GDPR, including the right to data portability. Please contact us for more information about GDPR compliance.
